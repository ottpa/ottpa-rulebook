# Section 2 - YEAR END POINT FUND PAYOUT
## 2.1 - Points Fund Payout:
For a class to qualify for a year-end points fund payout, the class must have a minimum of 5 hooks and 3 or more vehicles competing for the championship. The payout is a pre-determined purse as outlined in the chart below. These funds are paid out at the annual awards banquet.
