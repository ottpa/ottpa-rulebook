# Section 8 - SPONSORSHIP DECAL REQUIREMENTS
OTTPA has worked to secure great sponsors and formed partnerships with these vendors to help support the OTTPA and the year-end points fund.

## 8.1 - Competitors are required to:
1.  Display all supplied sponsor decals on both sides of the pulling vehicle in a highly visible location.

2.  Supplied sponsor decals must be on the pulling vehicle before the vehicle completes a competitive pass down the track or the competitor will not receive any purse check or points for that event.

3.  A list of the required decals will be on display at the entry trailer.
