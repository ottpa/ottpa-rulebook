# OTTPA MEMBERSHIP
The OTTPA license is like a driver’s license. It will have the OTTPA logo, your name, address, social security number, class and insurance information. All competing memberships will be valid from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.

## Competing Membership & Insurance Fee & Dates:
- Can join the day of a pull - \$450.00

- Postmarked by 03/31/2026 \$350.00

<!-- -->

- Postmarked after 03/31/2026 \$450.00

## Competing Membership Includes:
- 2026 Competition License Card

- 2026 Insurance Card

- Two OTTPA Decals

- Four (4) All Access Pit Passes

## Vehicle Registration Fee
- Per Vehicle \$350.00

> **NOTE:** You must specify a class of registration for the vehicle when paying membership.

## OTTPA Points Fee
- Per Vehicle \$150.00

- Required to participate in the OTTPA points championship and receive points at each event to receive year-end awards and points fund payout.

## Insurance Coverage Summary
The following is a brief summary of insurance coverage for those who hold an OTTPA Membership:

- Insurance coverage at event site for any OTTPA sanctioned K&K-insured event includes: \$5 million liability; \$150,000 Medical and Dental; and \$25,000 Accidental Death and Dismemberment.

- Spouse and dependent children have AD&D, Medical and Dental coverage for accidents while on site at OTTPA sanctioned events when the promoter is using OTTPA/K&K Promoter’s insurance.

- For information on insurance coverage, loss reports, etc., refer all calls/correspondence to: K&K Insurance Agency, 1712 Magnavox Way, PO Box 2338, Fort Wayne, IN 46801. Phone 219.455.3000.

## Purse Winnings Checks – 180 Day Expiration
All competitors that receive a check for class purse winnings must deposit or cash the check within 180 days from the time that it is issued by the OTTPA. If not it is subject to be voided and a new check will not be issued.
