# APPLICATION OF RULES
When applying the rules outlined in the OTTPA Rule Book, there are three classifications of rules, each with a specific order of application:

1.  **General Rules**: These apply to all members across all classes.

2.  **Truck or Tractor General Rules**: These build upon and clarify the General Rules, depending on the type of pulling vehicle.

3.  **Class-Specific Rules**: These further refine and add to the Truck or Tractor General Rules for each specific class.

In some cases, divisional or class-specific rules may override General Rules, either permitting or prohibiting something that the General Rules address differently.
