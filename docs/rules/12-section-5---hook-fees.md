## 5.1 - Hook Fee Information
1.  Members will pay an entry fee using the chart above.

2.  1-time hook fee is \$175.00 and the competitor will receive only 2 pit passes.

3.  If you are not registered two (2) hours before the starting time of the pull, you will receive one warning and pay a \$50.00 extra hook fee for the day, in addition to the regular hook fee. If you are late after the first warning, you will have \$100.00 deducted from your payout for that day.
